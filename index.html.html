The Tale of Ajay

Welcome to my realm of creation. On my Instagram channel, a character named Ajay takes you on a spiritual journey. While he paints intricate scenes of ancient Indian mythology—from Nandi Maharaj to Shri Krishna—he narrates the timeless stories behind them.


Every brushstroke is an homage to our heritage, breathing life into the legends of Mount Kailash and beyond. Follow the journey to watch these stories unfold on canvas.
